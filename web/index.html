Ask first

git safe owner/repo checks the shared database and gives you the pithy summary: scans done, vulnerabilities found.

Scan with your key

Want a fresh look? It clones shallow, pre-filters the risky files, and reviews them with the OpenRouter model of your choice — on your key.

3

Share the counts

The result lands in the public database so the next person sees the summary before spending a cent. Finding details stay with you.

The deal

  • Your key stays yours. It's sent only to OpenRouter, from your machine. The server never sees it.
  • Counts are public, details aren't. The database shares scan counts and totals; full finding lists stay in your local report.
  • Public repos only. git safe refuses anything that isn't publicly cloneable.
  • LLM findings ≠ truth. Results are model-generated leads, not an audit. Verify before you panic.
